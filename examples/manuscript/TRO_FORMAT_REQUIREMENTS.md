# IEEE Transactions on Robotics format checklist

Verified from IEEE/RAS official pages on 2026-08-12.

## Manuscript type and source

- Use **Regular Paper** for this project.
- LaTeX class: `\documentclass[journal]{IEEEtran}`, default 10 pt.
- IEEE Transactions two-column layout; no cover page and no custom crowded header/footer.
- Official blank package: `official_template/IEEE_Transactions_LaTeX_blank.zip`.

## Initial submission

- Maximum 18 pages for a regular paper, including figures, tables, and references.
- Double-anonymous PDF: no author names, affiliations, acknowledgments, funding, biographies, identifying links, logos, or author metadata.
- Abstract: at most 200 words. PaperCept metadata also limits the abstract to 1200 characters. This draft is 130 words and about 1049 characters before any later revision.
- Include index terms on page 1. PaperCept requires exactly four keywords; three must be selected from its T-RO list.
- References must list complete authors rather than shortening bibliography entries with “et al.”
- Minimum acceptable PDF version is 1.4; check embedded fonts using the T-RO PaperCept PDF checker.
- T-RO does not accept ordinary supplementary files. The paper must be self-contained.
- Optional multimedia is distinct from supplementary material. It must be submitted for review as one ZIP of at most 50 MB, with `ReadMe.txt` and a no-more-than-five-sentence `Summary.txt`.

## Accepted final version

- Maximum 20 pages. Pages 13–20 incur a mandatory USD 175 charge per page.
- Restore authors, affiliations, correspondence, funding, and acknowledgments.
- Add author biographies and photographs; each biography is at most 100 words.
- Submit the final PDF and complete source/figure package within 30 days of acceptance.

## Graphics

- Preferred vector formats: PDF/EPS/PS with embedded fonts.
- Raster color or grayscale: over 300 dpi; monochrome line art: over 600 dpi.
- Nominal widths: 3.5 in (88.9 mm) single column or 7.16 in (182 mm) double column.
- Figure text should remain approximately 9–10 pt at final size.
